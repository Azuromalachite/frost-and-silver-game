# Weather presets: Standard, Hard, Extreme (2026-11-18)

The intro offers four kinds of weather: Random (new weather each game), Standard (seed 12345), Hard (seed 313) and
Extreme (seed 2024). **Difficulty changes only the weather sequence.** The weather model (`rollYear`), yields,
tribute, the mita and every historical assumption are the same in all four. A preset is a seed: any run, preset or
not, can be replayed from its number alone with `?seed=` in the address, and the end screen and the copied report
name both the preset and the seed.

The presets are sequences drawn from the game's own weather model and chosen by the screen below. They are not
records of the weather in 1500–1600, and the game says so where the player chooses one.

## How the seeds were chosen

`tools/seeds.html` played seeds 1–2000 on main `069102e`, and the finalists again on `074861a` (identical
numbers). For each seed:

- **the default plan**, as a player leaves it pressing Next step, choosing "Best land first" at each re-plan (the
  pinned run's rule), and again choosing "Keep the same shares";
- **all 2,304 tested plans** (the end screen's grid), keeping the best by whole-game welfare, ties to fewer deaths.
  That plan is picked after the weather is known, so it is a hindsight benchmark, not a fair score.

Counting droughts alone would not do: a good Hard seed is one where an inexperienced plan does badly **and** a
competent plan can still do well, so the game rewards learning rather than only punishing.

Over the 2,000 seeds (5th / 25th / 50th / 75th / 95th percentile):

| | 5 | 25 | 50 | 75 | 95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| default plan, colonial food (kcal a day, harvests 4–6) | 976 | 1,236 | 1,390 | 1,515 | 1,683 |
| default plan, hunger deaths | 0 | 12 | 36 | 66 | 122 |
| best tested plan, colonial food | 1,283 | 1,494 | 1,624 | 1,709 | 1,714 |
| welfare, best tested minus default | 7.9 | 13.6 | 19.4 | 24.6 | 31.3 |
| "Keep the same shares", colonial food | 641 | 838 | 989 | 1,136 | 1,334 |

Hard candidates: default-plan colonial food 850–1,150 kcal, at least 60 hunger deaths; best tested plan at least
1,400 kcal with at most 40 deaths; a welfare gain of at least 18. Forty seeds passed. The finalists were then played
with one **fixed general plan chosen without seeing the weather** (Valley terraces, one Inca chuño crew, best land
first; after 1573 no chuño or wage crews, best land first), the best plan of the strategy sweep
([strategies-2026-11-18.md](strategies-2026-11-18.md)):

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12345 (Standard) | 53 | 1,411 / 27 / 67.4 | 1,527 / 8 / 76.9 | 78.1 | 10 | 1 |
| **313 (Hard)** | 9 | 1,067 / 92 / 45.7 | 1,487 / 4 / 71.1 | 71.1 | 12 | 181 |
| 833 | 13 | 1,104 / 66 / 49.5 | 1,573 / 12 / 83.1 | 83.1 | 5 | 142 |
| 1999 | 10 | 1,072 / 71 / 52.7 | 1,558 / 15 / 82.6 | 82.6 | 6 | 96 |
| 49 | 6 | 1,014 / 82 / 48.2 | 1,430 / 37 / 77.7 | 77.7 | 4 | 139 |
| 592 | 5 | 979 / 103 / 44.9 | 1,439 / 35 / 79.4 | 79.4 | 3 | 235 |
| **2024 (Extreme)** | 0.15 | 548 / 217 / 36.1 | 900 / 133 / 54.4 | 55.4 | 59 | 247 |

- **Standard, 12345**, is a median weather (53rd percentile). It is also a weather where the default plan is hard to
  beat by much: one tested plan beats it by 10 welfare points.
- **Hard, 313**, at the 9th percentile, has the clearest rescue: the default plan loses 92 people to hunger, the
  general plan 4, and twelve plans come within 5 welfare points of the best, so there is more than one way through.
  Its colonial years have a drought in c. 1575 and hard winters after c. 1592 and c. 1600.
- **Extreme, 2024**: three seeds of 2,000 are worse for the default plan, and only four have a lower best-plan food
  than its 1,011 kcal. Every tested plan loses at least about 130 people, so Extreme is about survival under severe
  limits, not about reaching the goal.

On almost every Hard candidate the best tested plan is the general plan above. Hard Mode therefore rewards what a
student learns on Standard weather; it does not need a different strategy.

## What the end screen does with it

- **Community survival** opens the success box. For a preset it gives the default plan's food and hunger deaths on the
  same weather as the yardstick, so choosing Hard or Extreme is never a penalty; for any weather it gives the seed to
  replay.
- **Decision quality** heads the comparison with the 2,304 tested plans on the player's own weather.

## Checks

`tests/review_model.cjs` re-measures each preset's default-plan food and deaths (`WX_PRESETS` in the game) on every
pull request, and its self-test plants a Hard preset pointing at seed 314, which must fail. A change to the model that
moves these numbers must re-run `tools/seeds.html`, and update `WX_PRESETS` and this file together; if the change
moves Hard out of its band, choose the seed again.

Limits: one plan grid that keeps one rule per period; welfare as the game defines it; 2,000 seeds; the 1,500 and
1,700 kcal lines are the game's own.
